# Cross-Product Launch Readiness Review

Sanitized template for a multi-product launch or migration gate.

## Readiness dimensions

| Dimension | Exit evidence | Accountable function |
|---|---|---|
| Product | Scope, experience, and exception paths approved | Product |
| Engineering | Testing, capacity, observability, and rollback ready | Engineering/SRE |
| Data | Reconciliation and success metrics validated | Data/Analytics |
| Legal and compliance | Required reviews complete; open conditions explicit | Legal/Compliance |
| Customer operations | Support playbook, escalation route, and staffing ready | Support/Account teams |
| GTM and enablement | Audience, messaging, documentation, and training ready | Marketing/Enablement |

## Gate decision

- **Go:** all critical evidence complete; residual risks accepted by named owners
- **Conditional go:** bounded gaps have owners, deadlines, and tested mitigations
- **Hold:** a customer, revenue, compliance, security, or rollback guardrail is not met

## Migration-wave review

Record cohort size, eligibility rules, exclusions, leading indicators, stop conditions, support capacity, and the evidence required before increasing the next wave.

> Sanitized artifact: representative decision structure only; product-specific thresholds and internal controls are omitted.
