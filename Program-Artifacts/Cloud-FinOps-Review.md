# Cloud FinOps Operating Review

Sanitized template for connecting cloud optimization to service and business outcomes.

## Review table

| Cost driver | Owner | Unit measure | Business guardrail | Action | Forecast impact | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compute workload | Team A | Cost per workload unit | Latency and quality | Rightsize | Range | Medium |
| Shared platform | Platform team | Cost per customer/product | Availability | Improve utilization | Range | High |
| ML training | ML team | Cost per training run | Model quality | Scheduling/architecture | Range | Medium |
| Inference | ML platform | Cost per prediction | Latency and revenue | Optimize serving | Range | Medium |

## Decision rules

- No savings proposal is approved without a service or revenue guardrail.
- Shared costs must have a documented allocation method and accountable owner.
- Forecast savings remain separate from realized savings.
- One-time reductions and durable unit-economics improvements are reported separately.
