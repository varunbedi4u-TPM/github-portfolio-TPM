# Enterprise AI Enablement

## Business problem

Teams were adopting AI-assisted work quickly, but useful context remained trapped in individual sessions. Knowledge was repeatedly re-derived, program questions still depended on a few experts, and adoption lacked a consistent quality and governance model.

## My mandate

Scale reusable AI knowledge practices across technical and non-technical teams and validate whether an internal assistant could answer complex program questions from governed source material.

## Scale and stakeholders

- Engineering and non-engineering teams across a global technology company
- Data Science, program teams, knowledge owners, and enterprise users
- A live program spanning seven workstreams and 150+ contributors as the pilot context

## Operating mechanism I created

- A knowledge-capture workflow that converted AI-assisted sessions into reusable organizational context
- A pilot evaluation model for program-status, milestone, decision, and history questions
- A feedback loop connecting poor answers to missing, stale, or weakly governed source material
- Phased adoption based on use-case value, answer quality, permissions, and operating risk

## Key decisions and trade-offs

- Treated source quality and ownership as prerequisites for scaling assistant access.
- Prioritized repeatable workflows over isolated demonstrations.
- Balanced wider reuse with the need to preserve access controls and context boundaries.

## Measurable outcome

- Expanded the knowledge-capture model from engineering toward company-wide use.
- Validated automated Q&A against a complex, multi-workstream program.
- Reduced repeated context reconstruction and the associated AI usage cost.
