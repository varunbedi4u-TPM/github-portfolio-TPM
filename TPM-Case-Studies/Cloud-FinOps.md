# Cloud FinOps and Cost-to-Revenue Governance

## Business problem

Cloud spend was visible in aggregate but difficult to attribute consistently to products, services, teams, and revenue outcomes. Optimization tended to arrive in reactive bursts when costs spiked, while inconsistent labeling and ownership made durable accountability difficult.

## My mandate

Create a cross-functional operating model that made cloud cost ownership measurable, connected technical efficiency to revenue, and built cost consciousness into routine engineering decisions rather than periodic cost-cutting exercises.

## Scale and stakeholders

- Infrastructure and ML workloads across model training, feature platforms, and inference
- Engineering, Infrastructure, Data/BI, Finance, and program leadership
- Multiple teams sharing common cloud platforms

## Operating mechanism I created

- Resource-labeling governance to establish per-team ownership
- A recurring cost review organized by cost driver, accountable owner, action, and expected impact
- A cost-to-revenue ratio developed with BI to measure efficiency in business context
- Layer-specific optimization tracking across training, feature computation, inference, and shared infrastructure
- Shared labeling standards that associated spend with products, services, environments, and accountable teams
- Common cost-efficiency goals and unit-cost measures so teams could optimize against the same definition of success
- Asynchronous action tracking and automated reminders designed to make cost management a sustained operating habit

## Key decisions and trade-offs

- Avoided treating cost reduction as an isolated target that could compromise revenue or reliability.
- Established ownership and measurement before demanding savings commitments from teams.
- Focused on durable unit economics instead of one-time budget cuts.
- Distributed optimization across engineering, ML, data, and infrastructure teams so cost ownership did not remain centralized in Finance or a temporary program office.

## Measurable outcome

- Cloud initiatives reduced annual GCP spend by $19M.
- Established per-team cost accountability through resource labeling.
- Created a cost-to-revenue measure that connected infrastructure efficiency to business performance.
- Transitioned cost tracking toward business-as-usual ownership and a shared engineering culture of continuous optimization.
