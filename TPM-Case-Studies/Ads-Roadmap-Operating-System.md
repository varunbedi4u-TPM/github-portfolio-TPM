# Ads Roadmap Operating System

## Business problem

A global Ads organization lacked a reliable portfolio view across specialized engineering teams. Incomplete roadmap data and inconsistent team-level processes limited leadership's ability to see dependencies, make resource decisions, and forecast delivery.

## My mandate

Create one governance system for roadmap planning, delivery health, and executive decision-making without forcing every team into an identical development process.

## Scale and stakeholders

- 600+ people across 20+ engineering pods in AMER and EMEA
- 2,000+ roadmap records
- Product, Engineering, Data, Finance, and senior leadership

## Operating mechanism I created

- A unified roadmap schema with required ownership, milestone, dependency, and status fields
- Automated data-quality and overdue-item alerts
- Portfolio views for teams, program leaders, and executives from the same source data
- An executive insight cadence that paired portfolio signals with recommended decisions

## Key decisions and trade-offs

- Standardized the minimum data needed for portfolio governance while preserving team-level delivery methods.
- Prioritized data integrity before adding more dashboards; incomplete inputs would only automate false confidence.
- Shifted executive reporting from activity summaries to decisions, consequences, and resource options.

## Measurable outcome

- Reduced missing roadmap data from 27% to 8%.
- Improved on-time delivery from 35% to 59%.
- Improved delivery predictability by 24% following automated alerting.
- Exposed a systemic dependency bottleneck affecting 42% of the portfolio and enabled a targeted resourcing response.
