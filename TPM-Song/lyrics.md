**[Verse 1]**
Monday morning, stand-up starts,
Ten new blockers, zero charts.
Product says, "We need it now,"
Engineering says, "Tell us how."
Three teams moving, none aligned,
Dependencies crossing every line.
I'm in the middle, making it work,
Finding the problem before it lurks.

**[Pre-Chorus]**
I don't write the code,
I don't own the app,
But when something breaks,
Guess who gets the Slack?
I connect the dots,
I keep the trains on track,
And when the plan goes sideways—
I'm the one bringing it back.

**[Chorus]**
Remember my name,
I'm the one behind the scenes,
Turning chaos into plans,
Turning blockers into dreams.
Remember my name,
When the deadline's getting tight,
I'm coordinating the battle
While you're asking, "Are we right?"
I may not push the code,
But I make the roadmap run,
When a hundred things collide—
I'm the reason it gets done.

**[Verse 2]**
Jira says the sprint is green,
But I know what that really means.
"Almost done" for three more weeks,
Five dependencies nobody speaks.
Architecture meeting, two hours long,
Everyone agrees… then something's wrong.
Someone says, "Let's circle back,"
I quietly update the action tracker tab.
Security needs another review,
Legal has a question, finance does too.
Engineering says, "We're good to go,"
Then production says, "Absolutely no."

**[Pre-Chorus]**
I don't need the spotlight,
I just need the facts.
Give me risks and owners,
And I'll build you a plan back.
I see the moving pieces,
I know where they connect,
And if there's one loose thread—
You know I'll find it next.

**[Chorus]**
Remember my name,
I'm the one behind the scenes,
Turning chaos into plans,
Turning blockers into dreams.
Remember my name,
When the deadline's getting tight,
I'm coordinating the battle
While you're asking, "Are we right?"
I may not push the code,
But I make the roadmap run,
When a hundred things collide—
I'm the reason it gets done.

**[Bridge]**
No authority,
But somehow all the responsibility.
No direct reports,
Still managing the whole damn sport.
Engineer says, "Ask the PM."
PM says, "Ask the tech."
Executive says, "What's the status?"
And I'm thinking, "What comes next?"
So I make the call,
I raise the flag,
I find the owner,
I close the gap.
Because programs don't manage themselves—
Someone's gotta make it happen.

**[Final Chorus]**
Remember my name,
When the launch goes live,
When the teams come together
And the whole thing comes alive.
Remember my name,
When the impossible gets done,
When the risks are all retired
And we finally say, "We won."
I may not write the code,
I may not own the team,
But I turn a thousand moving parts
Into one executable dream.
So when everything comes together,
And the credits start to roll—
You might not see me on the stage…
But I'm the one who made the whole thing go.

**[Outro]**
So remember my name.
Not because I wrote the code.
Not because I built the product.
Because I got everyone moving
in the same direction.
