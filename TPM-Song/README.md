# Remember My Name — The TPM Song

A resume can describe the outcomes. This song is about the work behind them: making technical risk visible, turning ambiguity into decisions, and helping independent teams execute as one.

---

**[Verse 1]**  
Monday morning, stand-up starts,  
Ten new blockers, moving parts.  
Product says, “We need it now,”  
Engineering asks, “What—and how?”  
Three teams moving, none aligned,  
Dependencies crossing every line.  
I make the hidden risks appear,  
Then turn the noise into a path that's clear.

**[Pre-Chorus]**  
I shape the trade-offs,  
I make dependencies clear.  
I turn technical risk  
Into decisions teams can steer.  
I connect the dots,  
I keep the outcomes on track.  
And when the plan goes sideways—  
I help the teams bring it back.

**[Chorus]**  
Remember my name,  
I'm the one behind the scenes,  
Turning chaos into choices,  
Turning blockers into dreams.  
Remember my name,  
When the deadline's getting tight,  
I'm aligning every team  
On what we choose—and why.  
I build the shared roadmap,  
So the teams can move as one.  
When a hundred things collide—  
The shared system gets it done.

**[Verse 2]**  
Jira says the sprint is green,  
But I test what “green” should mean.  
“Almost done” for three more weeks,  
Five dependencies no one sees.  
Architecture fills the room;  
I turn debate into decisions soon.  
Security, legal, finance too—  
Clear exit criteria pull us through.

**[Pre-Chorus]**  
I don't need the spotlight,  
I just need the facts.  
Give me risks and owners,  
I'll turn them into a plan.  
I see the moving pieces,  
I know where they connect.  
And if there's one loose thread—  
I'll flag it before it's a threat.

**[Chorus]**  
Remember my name,  
I'm the one behind the scenes,  
Turning chaos into choices,  
Turning blockers into dreams.  
Remember my name,  
When the deadline's getting tight,  
I'm aligning every team  
On what we choose—and why.  
I build the shared roadmap,  
So the teams can move as one.  
When a hundred things collide—  
The shared system gets it done.

**[Bridge]**  
No command chain,  
Still accountable for clarity.  
No direct reports,  
Still building shared reality.  
Engineers bring options,  
Product brings the bet.  
Leaders ask, “What's the status?”  
I answer, “Here's the choice ahead.”  
So we make the call,  
We raise the flag,  
We name the owner,  
We close the gap.  
Because programs don't manage themselves—  
The mechanism makes progress last.

**[Final Chorus]**  
Remember my name,  
When the launch goes live,  
When the teams move together  
And the whole thing comes alive.  
Remember my name,  
When the hard work's finally done,  
When the risks are understood  
And the outcomes have begun.  
I may not own the code,  
And no one person owns the team,  
But I turn a thousand moving parts  
Into one executable dream.  
So when everything comes together,  
And the credits start to roll—  
You'll see the teams that built it  
And the system that made them whole.

**[Outro]**  
So remember my name.  
Not for being the hero.  
Not for owning every answer.  
For getting everyone moving  
in the same direction.
